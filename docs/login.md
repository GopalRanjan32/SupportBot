# Login and Account Access

## Logging in to NoteNest

Users can log in to NoteNest using their registered email address and password.

To log in:

1. Open the NoteNest login page.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Enter your password.
4. Click "Log In".

## Forgot Password

If you forget your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login page.

NoteNest will send a password reset link to your registered email address.

The reset link expires after 30 minutes.

## Login Attempts

After 5 unsuccessful login attempts, the account is temporarily locked for 15 minutes.

Users can try logging in again after the lock period ends.
