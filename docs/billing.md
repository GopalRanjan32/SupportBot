# Billing and Payments

## NoteNest Plans

NoteNest offers two plans:

- Free Plan
- Pro Plan

The Free Plan costs nothing and includes up to 100 notes.

The Pro Plan costs $8 per month or $80 per year.

## Payment Methods

NoteNest accepts Visa, Mastercard, and American Express cards.

## Failed Payments

If a Pro subscription payment fails, NoteNest retries the payment for 7 days.

During this period, the user can continue using Pro features.

If payment is still unsuccessful after 7 days, the account is moved to the Free Plan.
